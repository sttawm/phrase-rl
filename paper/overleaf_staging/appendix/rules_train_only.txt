===RULES===

1. OUTPUT FORMAT. Your entire output is exactly one line: the final instruction text and nothing else. No reasoning, no explanation, no quotation marks, no labels like "Rewritten:" or "Output:", no alternatives, no blank lines before or after. When any rule below says copy unchanged, the copy is byte-for-byte: keep the input's spelling (even apparent typos like "srewdriver" or "banner"), capitalization, punctuation, articles, and tense.

2. FROZEN INPUTS — copy unchanged and stop. If the input is not in English (or you are unsure), OR it has no verb at all (a fragment like "lever vertical to front" or "right pepper shaker"), OR it contains an underscore, a digit inside a word, or an identifier-like token (e.g. "brown1fbox"), copy it byte-identical. Never translate, never repair spelling or grammar, never add articles, never guess what a fragment "means".

3. COPY-UNCHANGED DEFAULT. Run this checklist on the input; if ALL pass, copy it byte-identical and stop:
   (a) it is 15 words or fewer;
   (b) it begins with an action verb from this list, or a past/-s/-ing form of one: move, put, place, take, pick, grab, get, set, push, pull, slide, open, close, shut, fold, unfold, flip, turn, rotate, stack, remove, transfer, lift, drop, drag, bring, hold, keep, leave, wipe, sweep, lever, topple, upright, zip, unzip;
   (c) none of the deletion or repair triggers in Rules 5-12 fires on it.
   A short verb-initial input passes this gate even when it contains a vague word like "object", "thing", "figure", or "vessel" — such lines are normal canonical register; do not "fix" them.
   GLOBAL FALLBACK: whenever a trigger is ambiguous, you cannot parse the sentence, or two rules seem to conflict, copy the input unchanged. An unedited instruction is never the worst outcome; a wrong edit often is.

4. EDITS ARE DELETIONS. Every rule below either deletes listed strings, substitutes under a strict license (Rules 9, 11, 13), or rebuilds under Rule 12. Never reorder clauses, never paraphrase, and never add any word the input does not contain, except the trace-supplied name licensed by Rule 9 and the template words licensed by Rule 12.

5. STRIP THE REQUEST WRAPPER. Delete politeness and addressee framing wherever it appears: "please", "kindly", "thanks", "thank you", "for me", "robot,", "hey", "go ahead and", "just", "make sure to", "could you", "would you", "can you", "would you mind", "I want you to", "I need you to", "I would like you to", "if you would be so kind", "be so kind as to", "I would be grateful if you could", "if it is not too much trouble", "without delay". If the sentence was a question, also delete the trailing "?" so it reads as a bare imperative. If a deletion would leave a fragment with no verb, copy the original unchanged instead. Fix only the capitalization of the new first word; touch nothing else.

6. DELETE PURPOSE, MANNER, AND PRECISION. Delete: (a) purpose clauses starting "so that", "in order to", "to ensure", "with the objective of", "for the purpose of" — the whole clause, to the end of the sentence or the next comma; (b) manner adverbs anywhere: carefully, gently, slowly, quickly, neatly, cautiously, delicately, smoothly, swiftly, promptly, steadily, securely; (c) the precision words exactly, precisely, directly when they modify a placement ("exactly in the middle" becomes "in the middle"). Keep the input's own preposition and location words: never rewrite "in the center of the X" or "in the middle of the X" to "on the X" — center/middle goals are normal corpus register; only the precision word is deleted.

7. ORIENTATION WORDS — GUARDED DELETE. Delete "upright", "flat", "standing up", "on its side" ONLY when BOTH hold: the main verb is from the put/place/move/set/take/pick/grab/get family, AND the word is an extra placement adverb ("place it upright inside the ..."). NEVER delete it when it is the verb or the commanded goal state: "flip cup upright", "upright the basil bottle", "turn it on its side" keep every word.

8. NEVER ADD, NEVER SWAP.
   (a) Never insert a color, size, material, brand, orientation, or spatial word the input lacks — even if the scene trace confirms it is accurate.
   (b) Never swap any object, container, or surface noun for a synonym, a "more standard" word, or a category word: plate stays plate (never dish); cloth, towel, napkin, and rag each stay themselves; burner, stove, and cooker each stay themselves; and so on for every noun, including unfamiliar ones. There are no exceptions.
   (c) Never delete a color word, brand word, or object noun the input has.
   (d) Relation and direction words are FROZEN: on, off, in, out of, from, to, into, onto, up, down, over, under, left, right, top, bottom, between, behind, above, below, beside, near. Never change one for another, never expand "on" to "on top of", never contract "on top of" to "on", never drop a goal tail like "of the table" or "of the burner".
   (e) Leave articles exactly as given: do not add "the" to a telegraphic line, do not delete one, do not convert "a" to "the" or back.

9. DESCRIPTION BECOMES NAME (with fallback). Trigger: an object is referred to by a description instead of a name — a noun phrase containing "used for", "designed for", "designed to", "typically", "traditionally", "device", "apparatus", "implement", "appliance", "receptacle", "-shaped", or a relative clause ("which is ...", "that is ...") saying what the thing IS. Repair: replace the entire descriptive phrase with the plain one- or two-word name of that object, taken in this order of preference: (i) a plain name for the same object appearing elsewhere in the instruction itself; (ii) the name the scene trace gives. Write it as "the <name>", keeping a color word only if the input attached that color to this object. If neither source names it confidently, copy the whole instruction unchanged — never guess a familiar-sounding name, and never write "object", "thing", or "item" as a fallback.

10. DELETE RIDER MARKERS ONLY. Where an object noun is followed by "that is", "which is", "that are", "which are", "located", "situated", "positioned", or "currently sitting", delete just those marker words, keeping the location phrase itself when the sentence stays grammatical: "lift the silver pot that is on the left burner" becomes "lift the silver pot on the left burner". Do NOT delete plain spatial phrases that carry no marker ("the can on the left" stays as written) — spatial wording is frozen outside a Rule 12 rebuild.

11. COLOR SIMPLIFICATION. Basic color list (all corpus-present): red, blue, green, yellow, orange, white, black, purple, pink, brown, gray, grey, silver, violet, beige, maroon, dark. If a color term is hyphenated or compound AND contains a basic color word, reduce it to that basic word. If a color word is not on the list and contains no basic word, copy it unchanged. Never replace a color with a different color (no maroon to red, no beige to white, no violet to purple), and never drop a color.

12. REBUILD — for ornate inputs only, checked AFTER Rules 5-11 have deleted what they can. Trigger: the line still (a) has more than 15 words; OR (b) has two or more commas; OR (c) opens with one of: "if", "should", "assuming", "given that", "provided", "irrespective", "focusing", "it is imperative", "i would", "with regards to", "notwithstanding", "operating under"; OR (d) contains any of: subsequently, facilitate, orchestrate, configuration, adjacency, end-effector, manipulators, maneuver, trajectory, vicinity, workspace, relocation, directive. Repair: write ONE imperative of at most 12 words in the template VERB + "the" + OBJECT + PREPOSITION + "the" + GOAL. VERB from: put, place, move, take, pick up, grab, open, close, fold, unfold, flip, stack, push, pull, remove — matching the action described. PREPOSITION from: on, in, into, to, out of, from, off — matching the input's meaning; when the input names the relation, keep its word (Rule 8d). OBJECT and GOAL are the plain names of the things involved, copied from the instruction itself or licensed by Rule 9 from the trace — never invented, never synonyms. Keep a color the input attached to those nouns; drop all other qualifiers, conditions, and explanations. If the input commands two actions, output two such clauses joined by "and", in the input's order, each at most 12 words. If you cannot identify the action or the objects confidently, copy the input unchanged.

13. PLAIN VERB MAPPING. Apply only inside a Rule 12 rebuild, or when the input's main verb is itself on the left side of this table: relocate / reposition / transport / convey / shift → move; position / situate / deposit / rest / arrange → put; retrieve / obtain / acquire / seize → pick up; grasp → grab; extract / withdraw → take out; exert force / apply pressure → push. Verbs already plain stay untouched: put, place, move, set, take, pick up, grab, get, push, pull, open, close, shut, fold, unfold, flip, slide, stack, remove, transfer, lift. Never substitute "drop" or "lay" for another verb.

14. MULTI-STEP. Keep every commanded action, in the input's order, joined by bare "and". Delete sequencing words: then, after that, first, next, finally, subsequently. Never split one instruction into two lines, never merge two actions into one paraphrase, never drop an action.

15. LENGTH CEILING. Except when copying unchanged, the output is never longer in words than the input, at most 15 words total, and a Rule 12 rebuild is at most 12 words per clause. Reach the ceiling only through the deletions and repairs licensed above — never by cutting object nouns, colors the input attached, commanded actions, or relation words.

16. FINAL SELF-CHECK before emitting: (a) exactly one line, instruction text only; (b) every object noun in the output appears verbatim in the input or was licensed by Rule 9 from the trace; (c) no color, size, material, brand, orientation, or spatial word was added; (d) every relation word matches the input's; (e) if no rule's trigger fired, the output is byte-identical to the input. If any check fails, emit the input unchanged.

===RATIONALE===

All numbers below were re-measured directly from results/rules_runs/v2distill/train_only/evidence.csv (4,544 proxy-scored phrases, 220 train tasks, 213 with originals; no gt_success column exists) and corpus_stats.md (17,297 training instructions). "score" is the within-task 0-1 rank; "wz" the within-task z of the logit; deltas are logits. The proxy ranks phrases within a task — no number here is a success rate.

DATA NOTE (report upstream): train_only/06_mechanism_cases.md and 08_phrase_search.md are incompletely redacted val8 ROLLOUT data — every pure pair and Case in them maps to a val8 task (two ramekin pairs, bowl→cup and +upright, still survive in 08's table), and evidence.csv contains 7 val-side widowx_*_distract tasks that inherit phrases from held-out clean bases. No rollout percentage from those files is cited anywhere in this book; every rule stands on the train-side proxy and corpus statistics below.

- R2 (frozen inputs): verbless fragments ("lever vertical to front", "right pepper shaker"), identifier tokens ("close brown1fbox flap"), and typo'd canonicals ("pick up red srewdriver") are real train task originals in evidence.csv; 1-2% of the corpus is French/Spanish/Portuguese and ~10% is past-tense narration (corpus_stats), so these forms are in-distribution and any "repair" is an unmeasured rewrite.
- R3 (copy default): originals score mean 0.996 / median 1.00; the original is the top-ranked phrase in 182/213 tasks and is beaten by more than 0.5 logit in exactly 1 of 213 — edits to an already-plain input are downside-dominated. Originals average 5.5 words, 85% at 7 or fewer. Vague superordinates (object 1,553, thing 320, figure 180, vessel 163) appear in roughly one canonical line in seven or eight, so a category word alone must never trigger a rewrite; the category-word penalty (12% of phrases, wz −1.12) belongs to the ornate register Rule 12 handles.
- R5 (wrappers): the corpus contains zero politeness or second person (please/kindly/you/could/would all below the 5-count floor), yet polite natural rewrites score 0.78 vs 0.75 for other naturals — stripping the wrapper is a safe length normalizer, not a rescue. The danger the strip removes is length: within-task length-logit correlation averages −0.63 and is negative in 98% of tasks.
- R6 (purpose/manner/precision): purpose clauses wz −1.47 (n=57); manner adverbs score 0.349 vs 0.660; exactly/precisely/directly score 0.288 vs 0.665. "middle" (827) and "center" (433) are corpus-present goal words, so only the precision word is deleted, never the preposition or the middle/center phrase.
- R7 (orientation guard): "flip cup upright" and "upright basil bottle" are canonical train originals and "upright" is corpus-present; deletion is licensed only as a placement adverb (thin evidence class — kept solely because it patterns with R6's manner/precision penalties, and guarded so no goal state can be deleted).
- R8a (never add): single-token insertion pairs within task — +small −0.81 (n=10), +diagonally −0.48 (n=4), +metal −0.42 (n=13), +silver −0.34 (n=10), +yellow −0.17 (n=33), +green −0.16 (n=24), +red −0.16 (n=14); +blue measures +0.11 (n=13), the lone positive, too weak to license a class that is negative in aggregate. No insertion class reliably helps.
- R8b (never swap): plate→dish single-token swaps −0.12 (n=12); across the 21 train plate-tasks, dish-phrases score 0.407 vs 0.841 for plate-phrases. The block→cube exception in earlier drafts rested entirely on val8 rollouts and is deleted; corpus has block 868 ≥ cube 713. Synonym families (cloth 4,102 / towel 1,218 / napkin 199 / rag 62; burner 1,941 / stove 994 / cooker 56) are from corpus_stats.
- R8d (frozen relations): on train task "take sushi off plate", "pick up the sushi on the plate" sits −8.7 logits below "Pick the sushi up off the plate." — one relation word flips the task. "top of" (3,403) is frozen in both directions.
- R8e (articles): "the" 39,715 vs "a" 432 in the corpus; +the insertion pairs measure −0.04 (n=69, neutral); the a→the conversion had causal support of about n=2 pairs and is dropped.
- R9 (descriptions): describing an object instead of naming it is the ornate register's core failure — e.g. train sushi tasks where "the item traditionally served with soy sauce" phrasings score near the task floor while plain "sushi" phrasings fill the top of the board; category-word fallbacks were rules-v1's worst logged failure, hence trace-name-or-copy-unchanged, never guess, never "object".
- R10 (riders): rider markers wz −0.70; confirmed single-edit train pair on "pick up pan from stove": "lift the silver pot that is on the left burner" 2.36 vs "lift the silver pot on the left burner" 4.47 (−2.11). Added-spatial insertion pairs measure ≈0.00 on train proxy (43 pairs), so plain spatial phrases are frozen, not deleted.
- R11 (colors): basic list is exactly the corpus-present color vocabulary (including maroon, beige, violet, dark, grey/gray, silver); cross-color mappings had no train-side support and are banned.
- R12 (rebuild): adversarial-register phrases average 0.293 within-task score vs 0.758 natural; wz by length bucket: +0.47 (≤6 words), +0.26 (7-10), +0.23 (11-15), then the cliff −0.64 (16-20) and −1.26 (21-30) — hence the 15-word gates in R3/R12 with deletion rules firing at any length, leaving no untreated band. Winners (score ≥0.97) average 7.1 words; losers (≤0.05) average 22.7. Trigger openers/words are drawn verbatim from measured loser phrases in evidence.csv ("Should your operational parameters permit...", "Focusing your attention...", "It is imperative..."); embodiment jargon (gripper/end-effector/manipulators) carries wz −0.45 (n=121). Rebuilding from the input's own nouns is supported by token overlap with the canonical: >75% overlap +0.78 delta-logit vs −0.15 below 50%.
- R13 (verbs): set↔put 0.00 (n=35) and put↔place +0.01 (n=87) — plain verbs are interchangeable and left alone (v1's "set" ban is repaired); put/place→drop −0.11/−0.14 and →lay −0.14/−0.15, hence the drop/lay ban. Corpus verb families: move ≈8,300, put ≈5,000, place ≈2,500, take ≈1,300, pick ≈1,000.
- R14 (multi-step): sequencing words (then, after, first, finally) are below or barely above the corpus floor; multi-clause corpus lines use bare "and" (3,560) with order carried by clause position.

===KEY EVIDENCE (distiller)===
- Originals are the measured ceiling: mean within-task score 0.996 (median 1.00), top-ranked in 182/213 tasks, beaten by >0.5 logit in exactly 1 — copy-unchanged is the default and every edit needs a crisp trigger.
- Length cliff: within-task length-logit correlation −0.63 (negative in 98% of tasks); wz +0.23..+0.47 through 15 words, then −0.64 (16-20 words) and −1.26 (21-30); winners average 7.1 words, losers 22.7 — gates set at 15 words, adversarial register (0.293 vs 0.758 natural) rebuilt to a ≤12-word template.
- Never add, never swap: single-token insertions all ≈0 or negative (+small −0.81, +metal −0.42, +silver −0.34, +yellow −0.17); plate→dish swap −0.12 (n=12) with dish-phrases 0.407 vs plate-phrases 0.841 on 21 train plate-tasks; block→cube exception deleted (val8-only evidence; corpus block 868 ≥ cube 713).
- Relation words frozen: on-vs-off single edit on train task "take sushi off plate" = −8.7 logits, the largest minimal pair in the diet.
- Riders and clutter: "that is/which is/located" markers wz −0.70 with a confirmed −2.11 train pair (marker deleted, location kept); purpose clauses wz −1.47; manner 0.349 vs 0.660; precision 0.288 vs 0.665; category words wz −1.12 but present in 1-of-7 canonical lines, so they trigger nothing on short verb-initial inputs.
- CONTAMINATION (reported upstream): train_only/06 and 08 are incompletely redacted val8 rollout data (all 18 pure pairs and Cases 1,3-7 map to val8 tasks; two ramekin pairs survive in 08), and evidence.csv carries 7 val-side widowx_*_distract tasks — every rollout pp in the candidates' rationales was leakage and none is cited in the book.
