## What to write

Three sections, in this order. Cite counts or proportions where you can, and say
when a figure is approximate rather than presenting an estimate as exact.

## Shape of the corpus
Typical instruction length; the usual sentence form; how instructions begin;
whether articles and determiners are usually present; how often instructions are
multi-step rather than single.

## Register
How objects get described — by category, colour, material, brand, function,
position. Which of those are common and which are rare. Quote real examples.

## Notable absences
Word classes or constructions that are conspicuously rare or missing given the
domain. Name specific words you looked for and did not find in the vocabulary
table, and say what that suggests about the corpus's conventions.

Describe only what the corpus contains. Do not recommend phrasings and do not
speculate about what the policy prefers — later steps decide that from measured
outcomes, and a prior introduced here would contaminate them.

Reply with the three sections as markdown, and nothing else.
