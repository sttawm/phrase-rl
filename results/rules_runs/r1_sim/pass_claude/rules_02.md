===RULES===
1. Rewrite the instruction as a short, plain imperative that keeps the same objects and goal.
2. Exception: if the instruction is already a plain imperative of about ten words or fewer that names each object by a specific everyday noun — a demonstrative ("that can") or a material or brand word ("aluminum can", "cola can") still counts as specific — output it unchanged apart from the substitutions rules 4 and 5 allow; do not swap its verb, synonyms, word order, or punctuation.
3. Keep the instruction's spatial relation exactly as given (on, in, into, onto, inside, across, to); never swap one for another, and never add position detail the instruction does not state, such as "the center of" or "the edge of".
4. Replace technical, rare, or roundabout object references with the plain everyday word: a ramekin or small ceramic dish becomes "the white bowl"; a described drink container like "the cylindrical cola container" or "the red tin of cola" becomes "the red coke can" (but leave "the cola can" or "the soda can" as written); eating silverware becomes "the spoon"; a textile, fabric, or woven material becomes "the towel"; a vague reference like "the orange vegetable" or "the yellow one" becomes the specific noun it points to.
5. Whenever a carrot, keyboard, or wheel appears with no color word, add its own well-known color — "the orange carrot", "the black keyboard", "the black wheel". Never add any other color the instruction does not state: no plate, cloth, block, or basket colors, and never add "red" to an instruction that already says "coke can" or "cola can".
6. Strip politeness, questions, second-person address, and narrative framing entirely, but keep any real constraint on the motion (such as "without moving the other objects") as a short clause.
