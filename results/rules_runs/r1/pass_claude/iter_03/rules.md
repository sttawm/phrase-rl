===RULES===
1. Output exactly one line containing only the final instruction — no headings, labels, reasoning, quotes, or any other text. Never output the words "Thinking Process".
2. Rewrite the instruction as a short, plain imperative that keeps the same objects and goal.
3. Delete politeness, requests, and framing ("please", "could you", "I would be grateful", "I need you to", "make sure", "for me") and delete precondition clauses ("if the small box is empty", "provided that", "assuming"); state the action directly instead of the resulting state ("make sure the box is closed" → "close the box"; "move the bowl so it is no longer on the plate" → "take the bowl off the plate").
4. When an object is described rather than named, call it by its plain everyday name and drop the description's other adjectives: "the hemispherical dish intended for liquids" → "the bowl"; "the tall boiling vessel" → "the pot", not "the tall boiling pot". Rename a noun only when the everyday word means exactly the same thing — "washbasin" → "sink", "carton" → "box", "refrigerator" → "fridge", "laundry machine" → "washing machine" — and if unsure, keep the instruction's own noun. Write "top" for "upper" and "bottom" for "lower", never hyphenated ("top left corner", not "top-left corner").
5. Keep every object, source, destination, landmark, and either/or choice the instruction states — "in the pot or pan" stays "in the pot or pan", "from the basket" stays, "the pot that's resting in the sink" stays — and never add a colour, size, material, location, or object the instruction does not state.
6. Drop a colour, size, or material word only when the instruction mentions just one object of that kind and the word is not needed to say which one ("the orange cup" → "the cup", "the purple beet" → "the beet"). Never drop a word that tells two similar things apart — "the green cube on the yellow cube", "the little spoon", "the brown box" stay as they are — and never drop the colour from a vague noun: "the red object", "the blue figure", "the green thing" keep their colours.
7. Keep the instruction's verb whenever it is an everyday verb — lift, set, place, shut, grab, drop, spread, flatten, shift, swing, stack, pop, toss stay exactly as they are. Only replace ornate verbs ("transfer", "relocate", "deposit", "convey", "hoist", "grip", "reorient", "elevate") with the closest of: move, put, take, pick up, open, close, fold, unfold, turn, push, pull, lift, flip, remove, set, grab.
8. If the instruction tells the robot to move its own hand, gripper, or arm from one object to another while carrying nothing, write it as "end effector transition from X to Y" with the stated X and Y ("Transfer the robot hand from the first piece to the second one." → "end effector transition from the first piece to the second one").
9. Before answering, compare your output with the input word by word and undo every change no rule required: keep the original articles ("put fork from basket to tray" keeps its bare nouns), keep "out of" rather than changing it to "from", keep "into" and "onto" as given, and add no hyphens. If no rule requires any change, output the instruction exactly as given.
